Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty
[Dad Joke Showdown](https://make.fun/c/u-mfnakmal-dad-joke-showdown-1d2ce3e5).

One dad joke of at most 200 characters. A judge model reads the jokes two at a time and picks the one that lands the bigger groan.

`main.py` (Python 3) writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I asked Dad why he always keeps a pencil behind his ear, and he said in case he needs to draw the line somewhere.

Run it locally with:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
